Our story

About HomePlate

HomePlate is a student-built recipe discovery website designed to make cooking at home feel simpler and more approachable.

What we're building

Our website brings together meal categories, searchable recipe ideas, restaurant-inspired alternatives, and a random recipe picker. The goal is to help visitors discover dishes they can prepare at home.

How it works

This first version uses HTML for structure, one external CSS stylesheet for design, and lightweight JavaScript for interactive features. We plan to expand the recipe collection and add more detailed cooking instructions in future versions.
